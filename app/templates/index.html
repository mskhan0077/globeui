💡 Nominal Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)

BlueGrid calculates the Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) and Cost of Service (CoS) over a 25-year project life using a Nominal cash flow model, rather than a simple flat-rate real model.

  • Diesel Fuel: Escalates at 7.0% annually (historical CAGR).
  • O&M: Escalates at 4.0% annually.
  • BESS Replacement: Deflates at -5.0% annually (falling battery costs).

Nominal Discount Rate Formula:

r_nom = (1 + r_real) * (1 + inflation) - 1

This ensures future operational costs are discounted accurately, correctly penalizing systems that rely on long-term diesel top-ups.
