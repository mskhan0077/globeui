How to use
  1. Drag to spin the globe; scroll or pinch to zoom.
  2. Click a country or region to fly to it.
  3. Look for white rings: they mark places you can open. Some contain more places inside.
  4. Click a ring to zoom in. If planning data exists for that place, the planner opens.
  5. In the planner, Evaluate a mix, Find the optimal mix, or Compare places. Switch places from its dropdown.
  6. Go back with × or the browser's Back button. Click empty globe to zoom out.
